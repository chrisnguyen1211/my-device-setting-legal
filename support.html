My Device Setting (Android)

Contact

Email us at the developer contact address shown on the app's Google Play listing. Please include your phone model, Android version and the steps that cause the problem.

Common questions

  • Why does Wi-Fi or Bluetooth open a panel instead of switching? Android doesn't let other apps switch them directly, so the app opens the system panel.
  • A control shows a lock. Tap Fix and grant the permission that control needs.
  • Go back to my old launcher. Android Settings › Apps › Default apps › Home app.
  • Cancel Pro. Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.
  • Restore Pro on a new phone. Sign in with the same Google account and open the app; Pro is restored automatically.
