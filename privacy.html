Privacy Policy

My Device Setting (Android, package com.dvrk.settingshome) · Effective 22 October 2026

My Device Setting ("the app") is published by DVRK ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we have no accounts, no ads SDK and no analytics, and we do not run a server that receives your data. Almost everything stays on your device.

1. Information that stays on your device

  • Your installed apps. As a home-screen launcher, the app reads the list of apps on your device to show, search and open them. This list is never sent anywhere.
  • Your notes, Modes, layout, theme, language and other preferences. These are stored in the app's private storage.
  • Device status. Battery, storage, memory, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, sound and display settings are read so the Control feed can show and change them. They are not sent anywhere.
  • Your privacy choices from the first-run screen. These are stored on the device.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's settings and notes in your own Google account backup. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; we cannot access it.

2. Information sent to third-party services

The app only contacts outside services when you use the feature that needs them:

FeatureServiceWhat is sent
WeatherOpen-MeteoApproximate coordinates (only if you allow location), or the city name you type, plus your language
Discover / newsGoogle News RSSYour language and region
Web searchGoogle Search in your browserThe words you search for
Pro purchaseGoogle PlayHandled entirely by Google Play. We receive only whether you own Pro, never your payment details.

Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

3. Permissions

  • Approximate location: only for local weather, and only if you allow it. Precise location is never requested.
  • Modify system settings, Do Not Disturb access, sync settings: to change brightness, screen timeout, ringer, auto-sync and Do Not Disturb when you tap a control or run a Mode.
  • Notifications, run at startup, alarms: to run scheduled Modes.
  • See all apps, request uninstall: to work as a launcher (show, open and uninstall apps when you ask).
  • Set wallpaper: to apply a wallpaper you pick.

Every permission is explained in the app before it is requested, and you can skip any of them.

4. Advertising and analytics

The app currently shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDK. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent in the app first.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone, apart from the requests described in section 2 that you start yourself.

6. Data retention and deletion

All app data is on your device. Clear it at any time in Android Settings › Apps › My Device Setting › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

We will post any change on this page and update the effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing, or see Support.
